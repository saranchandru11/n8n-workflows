What it checks, and why

GRC frameworks like Archer are built around one idea: nothing runs on trust alone — every risk and control needs an evidence trail. These three checks apply that standard to AI decision logs instead of compliance controls.

CHECK 01
Recorded rationale
Is there a clear, recorded reason for the decision — one specific enough that someone else could arrive at the same call from the log alone?
CHECK 02
Escalation fit
Was it escalated appropriately for its own stated risk level? A Critical item auto-closed with no human, or a Low item paging an engineer, are both control failures.
CHECK 03
Defensibility
Is there anything here that couldn't be defended if reviewed months later — missing actor identity, no timestamp, or reasoning resting on facts that were never in the source?
